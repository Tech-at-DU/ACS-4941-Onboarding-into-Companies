## Why you should know this

Most entry-level jobs are filled through people, not job boards. A referral can make you many times more likely to get an interview. Outreach feels awkward at first, which is why we do it **in class, together** — by the end of today you'll have sent 10 real messages.

## Overview: Why Warm Outreach Works (20 min)

- **Warm outreach** means contacting someone you share a connection with (school, mutual contact, community, their public work). Warm messages get far more replies than cold ones.
- **Referral math:** a few referrals usually beat 100 cold applications.
- **Anatomy of a message that gets a reply** (see [templates](../Assignments/Networking_Outreach.md#outreach-templates)):
  1. Personal hook — why *them*, specifically
  1. Who you are, in one line
  1. A small, specific ask (15 minutes, one question)
  1. Under 100 words
- **AI-written outreach gets ignored.** People receive many generic, over-polished messages now. Signs: perfect but empty compliments, "I hope this message finds you well," long paragraphs, no specific detail about the person.
- **Good AI use:** researching someone's public work to find a genuine hook, or cutting your own draft down to size. **You** write the final message.

## Activity: LinkedIn Quick Fixes (15 min)

Before people respond to outreach, they look at your LinkedIn. Fix the highest-impact items now:

1. Headline: what you do + what you want ("Full-stack developer · React, Node · Open to junior roles")
1. About: 3–5 sentences in your own voice, with your live project link
1. Featured: add your proof-of-work project
1. Custom URL

Swap profiles and give one piece of feedback each.

## Outreach Power Hour (50 min)

Goal: **10 real, personalized messages sent before you leave.**

1. **Find people (10 min).** Use your target company list. Prioritize warm connections: ACS alumni, contacts the instructor suggests, people whose work you can reference.
1. **Write and send (35 min).** Write each message yourself. Read your first two drafts aloud to the group — classmates and the instructor give quick feedback before you send. Then keep going.
1. **Log every message (5 min)** in your tracker with a follow-up date one week out.

**AI in practice:** You may ask an AI tool to help you find a genuine connection point ("Summarize this engineer's recent blog posts in two sentences"). Verify what it tells you. Don't send any AI-drafted message without rewriting it.

## Activity: Review, Follow-Up Plan & Warm Intros (25 min)

1. Each student reads their best message aloud. What made it good?
1. The instructor reviews everyone's target companies and offers warm introductions from the ACS network where possible.
1. Plan informational interviews: when someone replies "yes," how will you schedule it? Draft your reply and thank-you note templates now.
1. Set your outreach target for the next week (suggested: 10 more messages).

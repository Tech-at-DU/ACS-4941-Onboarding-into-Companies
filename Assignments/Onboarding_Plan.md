# 30-60-90 Day Onboarding Plan

**Weight:** 5% · **Due:** Session 14 (Wed 12/9)

## Why

Getting the job is the start, not the finish. New engineers who ramp up quickly earn trust, get better projects, and are safer when budgets get cut. A 30-60-90 plan is a written plan for your first 30, 60, and 90 days. Some candidates bring one to final interviews. It also gives you a way to show your manager that you're intentional about growing.

## Requirements

Write a plan for a real or realistic role (use a job posting you're targeting). Aim for one to two pages.

### Days 1–30: Learn

- How you'll get your development environment working and ship a small first change (often a tiny bug fix or documentation update)
- How you'll learn the codebase: reading code, tracing one request end to end, reading past PRs (pull requests — proposed code changes submitted for review)
- Who you'll meet: your manager, your onboarding buddy, teammates, and a few people on other teams
- What you'll find out about the team's AI tool policy (approved tools, what code can be shared with AI, how AI-generated code is reviewed)
- How you'll track questions and decide when to ask vs. keep digging (a common rule: try for 30–60 minutes, then ask with what you've already tried)

### Days 31–60: Contribute

- What kinds of tasks you'll take on independently
- How you'll get feedback on your code reviews and work
- One way you'll add value beyond your tickets (improving docs, adding tests, fixing a flaky test, improving onboarding notes for the next new hire)

### Days 61–90: Own

- A small feature or area you aim to own
- How you'll take part in team processes (on-call rotation, sprint planning, code review for others)
- How you'll check in with your manager on your progress and goals

### Also include

- Three questions you'd ask your manager in your first 1:1 (one-on-one meeting)
- How you'll keep a "brag document" — a running list of what you've shipped and learned, for performance reviews and your resume

## Grading

| Criteria | Points |
| --- | --- |
| All three phases covered with specific, realistic actions | 50 |
| Includes AI tool policy questions and plan | 15 |
| Includes 1:1 questions and brag document plan | 15 |
| Tailored to a real role and company type | 20 |

# Resume & LinkedIn

**Weight:** 5% · **Due:** Session 5 (Mon 11/2)

Check at least 70% of the boxes on each checklist below. Bring both to Session 3 for a live review.

## Resume Checklist

### Format & structure

- [ ] One page
- [ ] Simple, single-column layout. No tables, text boxes, icons, or images that can confuse ATS software (applicant tracking systems — the software companies use to store and search applications)
- [ ] Saved as PDF with a clear filename: `FirstName-LastName-Resume.pdf`
- [ ] Standard section headings: Experience, Projects, Skills, Education
- [ ] Contact line: name, email, phone, city/state (no full address), LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio or live project URL
- [ ] Consistent dates, fonts, and spacing

### ATS & screening check

- [ ] Copy-paste your resume PDF into a plain text editor. Does all the text come through in the right order? If not, simplify the layout.
- [ ] Your resume clearly matches the 3–4 core requirements of a target job posting, using the posting's words where they're honestly true
- [ ] Skills section lists technologies you can actually discuss in an interview

### Content

- [ ] Your proof-of-work project is listed with its live URL
- [ ] Each bullet starts with a strong, plain verb (built, fixed, designed, reduced, tested — not "spearheaded" or "leveraged")
- [ ] Bullets show results with numbers where possible ("reduced page load from 4s to 1s," "used by 30 students")
- [ ] No buzzword filler, no "passionate about," no AI-sounding phrasing (see below)
- [ ] Every claim is true and something you can explain in detail
- [ ] Non-tech experience is reframed to highlight transferable skills (customer communication, teamwork, responsibility)

### AI check

- [ ] If you used AI to help write or edit your resume, you rewrote it in your own voice
- [ ] You asked an AI tool to critique your resume against a real job posting and addressed the useful feedback
- [ ] A classmate read it and could tell what you actually did in each role

**AI-sounding red flags:** "spearheaded," "leveraged cutting-edge technologies," "drove impactful results," "passionate," "dynamic," "synergy," "robust and scalable solutions," and three-adjective stacks.

## LinkedIn Checklist

- [ ] Professional photo (clear face, plain background — doesn't need to be formal)
- [ ] Headline says what you do and what you want: "Full-stack developer · React, Node, Postgres · Open to junior roles" — not "Student at…"
- [ ] About section: 3–5 short sentences in your own voice, with a link to your live project
- [ ] Featured section: your proof-of-work project and GitHub
- [ ] Experience and projects match your resume
- [ ] Skills section has your core technologies
- [ ] "Open to work" turned on (visible to recruiters only, if you prefer)
- [ ] Custom LinkedIn URL (`linkedin.com/in/yourname`)
- [ ] At least 50 connections, including classmates, instructors, and ACS alumni
- [ ] At least one post or comment showing your work (a project demo, a short write-up, something you learned)

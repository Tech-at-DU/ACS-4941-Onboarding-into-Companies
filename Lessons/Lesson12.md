## Why you should know this

This is the closest thing to a real final round you'll get before the real thing. It combines everything from the term: your pitch, your project, a technical problem, behavioral stories, and your questions for the interviewer. Doing it once under realistic pressure, with detailed feedback, is one of the most valuable things in the course.

## Learning Objectives

1. Perform a complete, multi-part interview under realistic conditions
1. Present your proof-of-work project and defend its decisions
1. Apply the technical interview sequence and STAR method under pressure
1. Identify your top strengths and areas to improve from structured feedback

## Standup (10 min)

What I did · What I'll do · Where I'm stuck. Confirm the loop order and that everyone has submitted their self-review.

## Full Mock Loops (3 × 45 min)

Each student completes a 45-minute loop with the instructor (and an alum or guest interviewer when available), tailored to the job description they brought. See the [Mock Interview Loop assignment](../Assignments/Mock_Interview_Loop.md).

| Segment | Time | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opener | 5 min | "Tell me about yourself" + "Why this role?" |
| Project deep-dive | 10 min | Walk through your proof-of-work project; expect "why did you…?" questions |
| Technical | 15 min | One problem chosen by the instructor: algorithm, debugging, or code review (no AI) |
| Behavioral | 10 min | Two or three questions, including one about how you use AI |
| Your questions | 5 min | You ask the interviewer questions |

**Run it like a real interview:** camera-ready, on time, no notes for the opener, plain editor for the technical part. Recording is encouraged (with permission) for your own review.

**While you're not interviewing:**

- Work on your [30-60-90 Day Onboarding Plan](../Assignments/Onboarding_Plan.md) (due Session 14)
- Prepare your research for Session 13's negotiation role-play (see [Negotiation assignment, Part 1](../Assignments/Negotiation.md#part-1-research-bring-to-session-13))
- Send follow-ups and log pipeline activity

**Instructor note:** if a guest interviewer is available, run two loops in parallel to free up time for an extra peer practice round. Score each loop on the [rubric](../Assignments/Mock_Interview_Loop.md#rubric-used-for-triads-and-the-graded-loop) and give each student written feedback within 48 hours.

## Group Reflection & Wrap Up (10 min)

- Each student shares one thing that went better than expected and one thing they want to work on.
- What was different about a full loop compared to the individual practice rounds? (Usually: stamina, and switching between modes.)

Before Session 13:

- Write your post-loop reflection: top 3 strengths, top 3 things to work on (part of the [Mock Interview Loop grade](../Assignments/Mock_Interview_Loop.md#grading))
- Complete your negotiation research — walk-away, target, and anchor numbers with sources
- Read the [Salary Negotiation Guide](../Resources/Salary_Negotiation_Guide.md)

# Job Search Plan & Weekly Pipeline Activity

**Weight:** 10% · **Plan due:** Session 2 (Wed 10/21) · **Pipeline activity:** reported at every session's standup

## Why

A job search without a plan turns into refreshing job boards and waiting. A plan turns it into a weekly routine you control. Employers can't control your outcomes, but you can control your inputs: messages sent, conversations held, applications tailored, and practice completed.

## Part 1: Job Search Plan (due Session 2)

Write a one-page plan (Markdown in your own repo, or a doc) that answers:

1. **Situation:** Are you job seeking, already employed, or looking to change roles? What's your timeline?
1. **Target roles:** List 2–3 role types you'll pursue. Look beyond "Software Engineer" (SWE): consider frontend/backend/full-stack developer, QA (quality assurance, testing software) or SDET (software development engineer in test, writing automated tests), solutions or support engineer, FDE (forward-deployed engineer, building solutions directly with customers), developer advocate, AI implementation roles, and contract or contract-to-hire roles.
1. **Target companies:** A starting list of 15 companies, sorted into tiers (dream / realistic / safe). Include at least 3 small companies or startups.
1. **Your pitch:** A two-sentence summary of who you are and what you're looking for.
1. **Weekly targets:** How many outreach messages, informational interviews, tailored applications, and practice problems you'll do each week. (Suggested minimum: 10 outreach messages, 1 informational interview, 5 tailored applications, 3 practice problems.)
1. **Work authorization:** If you need visa sponsorship or are on OPT (Optional Practical Training), note your key dates. (Private — share with the instructor only if you choose.)
1. **AI plan:** Which AI tools will you use for preparation, and for what? See [AI in the Job Search](../Resources/AI_In_The_Job_Search.md).

## Part 2: Weekly Pipeline Activity (every session)

Keep your [Job Search Tracker](../Resources/Job_Search_Tracker_Template.csv) up to date. Import the template into Google Sheets or Excel on Day 1.

At each session's standup, report three things in under 2 minutes:

1. **What I did** since last session (numbers: messages sent, replies, applications, interviews, practice)
1. **What I'll do** before next session
1. **Where I'm stuck**

If you're already employed, your pipeline focuses on informational interviews and relationship building rather than applications.

## Grading

| Criteria | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Plan covers all 7 sections with specific, realistic targets | 30 |
| Tracker kept up to date throughout the term (checked at random sessions) | 30 |
| Weekly targets met or honestly explained at standup (most weeks) | 30 |
| Plan revised at least once based on what you learned | 10 |

**Note:** you're graded on effort and consistency, not on whether you get replies or offers. Those are partly outside your control.
